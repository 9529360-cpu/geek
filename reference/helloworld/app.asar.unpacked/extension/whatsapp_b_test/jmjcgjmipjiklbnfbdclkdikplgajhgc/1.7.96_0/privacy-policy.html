Privacy Policy - WAPlus CRM for WhatsApp Web

Last updated: May 22, 2026

WAPlus CRM for WhatsApp Web ("WAPlus", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how the WAPlus Chrome extension (the "Extension") collects, uses, stores, shares, and otherwise processes information when you use it.

This policy is written to meet transparency expectations for the Chrome Web Store, including disclosure of the categories of user data we collect, how we use them, where they are stored, how long we keep them, and which parties may receive them, including service providers and user-directed destinations.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Extension distributed through the Chrome Web Store, the WAPlus services used by the Extension, and any in-product notices that reference this policy. The Extension is designed for use with WhatsApp Web at web.whatsapp.com.

This policy does not govern third-party websites or services that you access separately, including WhatsApp, Meta, Google, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, or webhook endpoints that you configure. WAPlus is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

2. Information we collect

We collect or process information only where needed to provide the Extension's CRM, messaging, automation, scheduling, translation, AI, synchronization, integration, support, security, and diagnostic features. Categories include:

2.1 WAPlus account and authentication data

  • WAPlus account identifiers, login status, authentication tokens, WAPlus identity cookies, subscription or plan information, workspace information, installation ID, Extension version, and related account metadata.
  • Collection: from your WAPlus account, browser cookie storage, Chrome extension APIs, and WAPlus services when you log in or use account features.
  • Purpose: authenticate you, connect the Extension with your WAPlus account, provide subscription features, maintain sessions, and provide support.

2.2 WhatsApp Web and CRM data

  • WhatsApp account ID, phone number, contact names, phone numbers, WhatsApp IDs, LIDs, group or chat names, profile photos or avatars, country code inferred from phone number, contact tags, custom tabs, custom fields, lead or customer profile fields, notes, reminders, imported contacts, and user-created CRM records.
  • Collection: from WhatsApp Web on your device and from information you enter, import, tag, sync, or save in WAPlus.
  • Purpose: provide CRM contact management, tagging, notes, reminders, tabs, contact import, search, synchronization, and support features.

2.3 Message metadata, content, and media

  • Message metadata may include message IDs, sender and recipient identifiers, chat identifiers, timestamps, message type, delivery or sync status, and related conversation metadata.
  • WAPlus does not automatically collect all WhatsApp message content. Message text, selected chat history, attachments, images, documents, audio, voice messages, or other media may be processed when you use features such as chat sync, conversation sharing, scheduled messages, bulk sending, quick replies, auto replies, AI chatbot, AI rewrite, translation, CRM note creation, third-party CRM integrations, media upload, or webhooks.
  • Purpose: provide the user-selected messaging, synchronization, automation, AI, translation, integration, and file handling features.

2.4 User-created CRM and automation data

  • Notes, tags, custom tabs, contact imports, reminder content, scheduled message content, quick reply templates, auto reply rules, broadcast lists, webhook settings, chatbot settings, AI knowledge sources, uploaded files, URLs, and integration configuration.
  • Collection: when you create, import, upload, configure, or enable those features.
  • Purpose: store your CRM workspace and operate automation, scheduling, broadcast, chatbot, and integration workflows.

2.5 Google integration data

  • If you enable Google Calendar or Google Contacts features, WAPlus may process Google OAuth authorization data, access tokens or authorization codes, calendar event titles, dates, times, locations, descriptions, contact sync settings, and contact data needed to provide the requested Google integration.
  • Purpose: create or manage calendar events and synchronize contacts only when you enable the relevant Google feature.

2.6 Third-party CRM integration data

  • If you connect HubSpot, Salesforce, or Zoho, WAPlus may process OAuth tokens and CRM records such as contacts, leads, deals, tasks, notes, calls, tickets, events, and selected WhatsApp messages or contact details that you choose to sync.
  • Purpose: provide the integration features you enable and keep WAPlus CRM data aligned with the selected third-party CRM.

2.7 Device, browser, telemetry, and diagnostics

  • Browser type, operating system, platform, user agent, language, timezone, feature usage events, error logs, request and response status, performance data, Extension version, installation time, random Extension instance identifiers, uninstall feedback metadata, WhatsApp version, and diagnostic information needed to maintain and improve the Extension.
  • Some diagnostic logs may include request payloads, response payloads, or feature context. If a feature processes message content, files, prompts, or CRM fields, relevant diagnostic records may include that information when needed to troubleshoot the feature.
  • Purpose: diagnose failures, monitor stability, improve compatibility with WhatsApp Web changes, prevent abuse, provide support, and improve product reliability.

2.8 Optional extension management data

  • If you grant the optional management permission, WAPlus may read installed extension metadata such as extension name, ID, version, enabled status, and related compatibility information.
  • Purpose: detect potentially conflicting WhatsApp-related extensions, help you disable conflicts, and troubleshoot compatibility issues.

2.9 User-configured webhook data

  • If you enable webhooks, you may configure one or more HTTP(S) endpoints. When triggered, WAPlus may send message-related or event-related payloads to those endpoints according to your configuration.
  • Important: those endpoints are not operated by WAPlus unless the URL is a WAPlus-owned endpoint. Data sent there is shared with the operator of each URL you configure.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  1. Provide the Extension's core functionality on WhatsApp Web.
  2. Authenticate users and connect the Extension to WAPlus accounts.
  3. Save, sync, and manage contacts, notes, tags, custom fields, reminders, templates, broadcasts, scheduled messages, conversation records, and automation settings.
  4. Provide optional Google Calendar, Google Contacts, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, webhook, AI, translation, chatbot, and file upload features when enabled by you.
  5. Store local preferences, tokens, caches, queues, templates, and logs using Chrome extension storage or browser local storage.
  6. Send telemetry and diagnostics to service providers for stability, security, support, and product reliability.
  7. Prevent abuse, enforce limits, and protect WAPlus and users.
  8. Provide customer support and respond to data requests.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Extension data for third-party advertising.

4. Permissions and network access

4.1 Chrome permissions

Permission Why it is needed
tabs and activeTab Locate, open, focus, and interact with WhatsApp Web and WAPlus pages when you start Extension features.
storage Store settings, tokens, preferences, templates, queues, local logs, and cached feature data.
cookies Read WAPlus authentication cookies, such as the WAPlus identity cookie, to detect login status and maintain Extension sessions.

contextMenus Provide right-click actions, such as sending selected text or phone numbers through WAPlus features.
alarms Run time-based tasks such as reminders, scheduled messages, background sync, and session checks.
management (optional) Detect extension compatibility conflicts if you grant this optional permission.

4.2 Host and service access

  • WhatsApp Web (https://web.whatsapp.com/*) - Required for the Extension to operate on WhatsApp Web.
  • WAPlus services (https://waplus.io/ and related WAPlus domains) - Used for authentication, CRM storage, sync, automation, subscription, support, and Extension APIs.
  • Google APIs - Used only when you enable Google Calendar or Google Contacts features.
  • HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho APIs - Used only when you connect the corresponding CRM integration.
  • Translation and AI endpoints, including Google Translate, Tencent Transmart, WAPlus AI endpoints, and AI or translation providers used by WAPlus - Used only for translation, AI rewrite, AI reply, chatbot, or related features you request.
  • Cloud storage and logging endpoints, including Alibaba Cloud OSS, CDN, and Log Service - Used for file storage, media delivery, logs, and diagnostics.
  • User webhooks - Any HTTP(S) URL that you configure for webhook delivery.

WAPlus does not monitor browsing activity on unrelated websites for advertising or profiling.

5. How we share information (recipients)

We disclose user data only as described in this policy, at your direction, to provide the Extension, or when required by law.

5.1 WAPlus websites and APIs

Recipient: WAPlus services hosted under waplus.io and related WAPlus-operated domains and infrastructure.

Role: operator of account, CRM, sync, automation, billing, integration, support, and Extension backend services.

Data may include: account data, authentication data, WhatsApp and CRM data, user-created records, message metadata, selected message content, media, files, automation settings, AI settings, integration settings, and diagnostic information.

5.2 Cloud storage, CDN, and logging providers

Recipient: cloud infrastructure providers used by WAPlus, including Alibaba Cloud services such as OSS, CDN, and Log Service.

Role: service provider or processor that stores, delivers, and processes files, media, avatars, attachments, telemetry, and diagnostic logs on our behalf.

Data may include: uploaded media, attachments, avatars, file metadata, event type, timestamps, install time, language, browser type, user agent, platform, Extension version, WhatsApp identifiers or phone number when available, country label derived from phone number, API diagnostic fields, and error details.

Not for sale: we do not permit these providers to use this data for their own advertising.

5.3 Google APIs

Recipient: Google APIs, including Google Calendar and Google Contacts, when you enable those features.

Data may include: OAuth authorization data, access tokens or authorization codes, calendar event details, contact sync settings, and contact data needed for the requested integration.

5.4 Third-party CRM providers

Recipients: HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho when you connect the relevant integration.

Data may include: OAuth tokens, CRM contact or lead data, notes, tasks, calls, deals, tickets, events, selected WhatsApp messages, and related records you choose to sync.

5.5 Translation and AI providers

Recipients: WAPlus AI services and translation or AI providers used to provide the requested feature, such as Google Translate, Tencent Transmart, and other AI providers used by WAPlus.

Data may include: selected message text, prompts, conversation context, translation source text, generated results, and user-provided chatbot knowledge sources, only as needed to provide the requested AI, chatbot, rewrite, or translation feature.

WAPlus does not use Extension data to train generalized AI or machine learning models for advertising or unrelated purposes.

5.6 Webhook endpoints you configure

Recipient: the operator of each webhook URL you enter, which may be you or a third-party vendor.

Role: user-directed transfer. WAPlus sends the configured payload to the endpoint you provide and does not control how that endpoint uses the data after delivery.

5.7 Legal, safety, and business transfers

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect rights, safety, security, users, WAPlus, or the public.

If WAPlus is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will continue to protect personal information consistent with this policy or notify users of material changes.

6. Google API Limited Use

WAPlus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google API data is used only to provide user-facing Google Calendar or Google Contacts features that you enable.
  • WAPlus does not use Google API data to train, develop, or improve generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • WAPlus does not transfer Google API data to third parties for AI or machine learning model training.
  • WAPlus does not use Google API data for advertising or unrelated profiling.

7. International transfers

Because WAPlus uses cloud providers, service providers, and domains that may process data in regions outside your country of residence, your information may be transferred across borders for the purposes described in this policy. We rely on appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law, such as contractual protections with processors and service providers.

8. Retention

Category Retention summary
Local Extension settings, preferences, cached identifiers, and local logs Retained on your device until you log out, clear browser or extension data, reset the relevant feature, or uninstall the Extension.
WAPlus account, subscription, and authentication records Retained while needed to provide your account, subscription, security, support, and audit functions, unless deletion is required or permitted by law.
CRM records, contacts, notes, tags, tabs, reminders, templates, automations, broadcasts, and conversation sync data Retained while needed to provide the service or until you delete the data, disconnect the feature, close the account, or request deletion, subject to legal, security, backup, and legitimate business requirements.
Uploaded files, media, avatars, attachments, and AI knowledge sources Retained while needed for the feature that uses them, until deleted by you, or until no longer needed for service, security, backup, or legal purposes.
OAuth tokens and integration credentials Retained while the relevant integration remains connected or while needed to complete the requested sync, unless you revoke access, disconnect the integration, or request deletion.
Telemetry, diagnostic logs, and API diagnostic records Retained for a limited period needed for debugging, stability, security, abuse prevention, support, and service improvement, then deleted or anonymized according to our log retention and backup practices.
Webhook payloads sent to user-configured URLs WAPlus sends the payload according to your configuration. Retention by the receiving endpoint is controlled by that endpoint's operator.

If you need a specific retention schedule for server-side data tied to your account, contact us at support@waplus.io.

9. Security

  • Local settings are stored using Chrome extension storage APIs or browser local storage on your device.
  • Remote calls use HTTPS/TLS where applicable.
  • Access to WAPlus systems, cloud logs, and service-provider consoles is limited to authorized personnel under access controls.
  • WAPlus uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or destruction.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Local data: you can clear Extension data in Chrome, log out, or uninstall the Extension.
  • CRM data: you can delete CRM records, notes, tags, templates, reminders, scheduled messages, broadcasts, and other user-created data where the product provides those controls.
  • Optional features: you can disable optional features such as chat sync, AI features, translation, automation, webhooks, and CRM integrations where available.
  • Integrations: you can disconnect Google, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, webhook, AI, or other integrations where the product provides those controls.
  • Google access: you can revoke Google permissions from your Google account security settings.
  • Requests: you may email support@waplus.io to request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data tied to your identifiers, subject to verification and legal exceptions.

11. Children's privacy

The Extension is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. We will revise the "Last updated" date and post the updated policy at the URL used in the Chrome Web Store listing or another official WAPlus policy URL.
